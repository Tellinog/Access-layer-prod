# AI Literacy

## Objective

Ensure staff and other people operating or using the AI system on behalf of the organization have role-appropriate knowledge of opportunities, risks, limitations and required controls.

## Roles

### Developers

- data boundary and provider risks;
- prompt injection and output validation;
- model/version change control;
- secure logging and evaluation.

### Operators

- configuration, kill switch and incident response;
- provider outage and fallback rules;
- telemetry without content capture.

### Reviewers/business users

- AI output is not automatically correct;
- required source and numeric checks;
- edit/reject/approval responsibility;
- prohibited downstream uses.

### Administrators

- capability and grant governance;
- provider/model approval;
- audit and export permissions.

## Evidence

Record materials, audience, date, completion/acknowledgement method, refresher schedule and owner. External certification is not assumed to be required; measures are proportionate to the system and role.
