# Intended Purpose

Status: `CURRENT_PRODUCT_PURPOSE_RECORDED; OWNER_APPROVAL_PENDING`

Access Layer centralizes Google Workspace identity verification, per-tool authorization grants, short-lived signed access tokens, activity-driven refresh sessions, administrative access management and safe audit records for internal UNGUESS tools.

It is not intended to make employment, credit, insurance, biometric, law-enforcement, medical or other high-impact automated decisions. It has no AI use case and must not be repurposed as an AI decision system without a separately reviewed scope. Named product and compliance owners are unresolved in `BACKLOG.md`.
