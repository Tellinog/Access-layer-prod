# Transparency and Provenance

## User notice

When applicable, disclose AI interaction clearly and accessibly at or before the first relevant interaction.

Recommended copy:

```text
AI-assisted draft
This feature uses AI to create a draft from the approved inputs shown here. Review the content and supporting sources before publishing.
```

## Output provenance

Approved metadata may include:

- AI-assisted flag;
- provider and pinned model ID;
- prompt/template version;
- generation timestamp;
- use-case/capability ID;
- input category summary, not raw content;
- source references;
- validation warnings;
- review and approval status;
- audit/result ID.

Do not expose:

- system/developer prompts;
- chain of thought;
- secrets or credentials;
- hidden security rules;
- sensitive source content.

## Machine-readable marking

Assess and implement Article 50 marking/detectability requirements where applicable to synthetic content. Record the technology, interoperability, limitations and exception analysis. A visible “AI-assisted” badge alone may not satisfy every machine-readable marking duty.

## Exported artefacts

PDF, document, presentation, image, audio, video or JSON exports include appropriate provenance metadata and visible disclosure when required by the use case and law.
