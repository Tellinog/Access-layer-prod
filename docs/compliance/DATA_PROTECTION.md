# Data Protection Linkage

## Purpose

Connect AI governance to the project’s GDPR/privacy obligations without pretending this repository is a complete privacy programme.

## Required record when personal data is processed

- data categories and subjects;
- purpose and necessity;
- controller/processor roles;
- legal basis to be confirmed by the responsible owner;
- data minimisation and retention;
- access, deletion and correction flows;
- provider/subprocessor and transfer assessment;
- security measures;
- DPIA screening and DPO decision;
- effect of logs, events, backups and generated exports.

## Deletion integrity

Delayed jobs, outboxes, analytics and AI artefacts must not recreate or re-export data after an approved privacy deletion.
