Before working on this repository:

1. Read `AGENTS.md`.
2. Follow the source-of-truth hierarchy defined there.
3. Do not duplicate or override project rules here unless Claude-specific behavior is required.

- Update `CURRENT_STATE.md`, `DEVLOG.md`, `BACKLOG.md` and `DECISIONS.md` according to `AGENTS.md`.
